### Buy You a Coffee

Whenever you’re in line at Starbucks, buy the person in front of you their order.

Firstly, it’s just nice and fun.

Secondly, it could become a viral social companion with very short chores that make people feel good.

It’s a good place to do product research.

What if you offered people free coffee at Starbucks in exchange for doing user research?

### Chrome Extensions as an AI Platform

Chrome extensions might be the best AI platform.

While Google, OpenAI, Perplexity, and others are off forking Chromium to build a browser, extensions offer native UX, zero friction, no vendor lock-in, and built-in distribution.

### Naming Is Context

Naming is more important than ever. It’s context. Good names reduce cognitive bloat for agents and humans.

### Semi-Automation

People love automation; I love semi-automation. It’s the parents’ principle on steroids.

### A CLI with an Embedded LLM

Every CLI should come with a small embedded LLM that explains how to use it and answers questions. It could use an embedded vector store or a sufficiently large context window.

### RAG and Context Engineering

RAG was a workaround for not having enough context. Now it is a solution for providing the right context. Context engineering matters.

### AI Enables Exploration and Execution

AI doesn’t just make existing work faster. It makes more experiments possible — including experiments you otherwise would not have attempted.

### TestFlight for Chrome Extensions
