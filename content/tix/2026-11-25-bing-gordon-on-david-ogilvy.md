In [The Knowledge Project #295](https://fs.blog/knowledge-project-podcast/bing-gordon/), Bing Gordon talks about what David Ogilvy taught his account executives: know the client's business better than anyone else in the room.

The episode also makes a case for spending more time on the headline. Gordon recalls that among people who remembered an ad, far more remembered the headline than the body copy. That's a useful test for writing a memo, too: if someone reads only the first line, what will they take away?
