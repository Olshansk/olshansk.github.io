I had a therapist when I was a kid. I had a therapist during COVID. I've had physical therapists throughout the years as well.

The thing about therapists is that you're not just going to go in and have someone solve your problems. You have to be open to having your problems solved. You need to be ready, but you also need to be on the same wavelength as the other person.

The other person has a life. They have priorities, and it is a transactional interaction. But even at work, you can love working with your colleagues. As long as you're aligned, it can work.
