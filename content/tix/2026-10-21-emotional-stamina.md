Sometimes, no matter how kind, thoughtful, helpful, and non-anxious you are, emotional stamina is a thing.

You hit issues that are frustrating, tough, challenging, and potentially even detrimental.
If you sense that your emotional battery is going down, it's time to take a break rather than keep pushing.

Why?
Because when you push through physically, even though sometimes you shouldn't, you will only impact yourself.
But when you push past your emotional stamina, you're going to impact those around you.

If you're an introvert, this goes even further, but I think it applies to everyone.
