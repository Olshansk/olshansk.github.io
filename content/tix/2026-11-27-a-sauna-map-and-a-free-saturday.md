Today I found [etsauna](https://etsauna.com/?lat=47.65732&lng=-122.22462&zoom=13), a map of public saunas and bathhouses. You can see prices and filter for things like a cold plunge or a wood stove.

Sites like this used to take a ton of effort to design, build, and keep up to date. Now, sometimes a free Saturday is enough to get a useful first version out the door. That used to feel like signing up for months of work.

Side projects don't need to turn into jobs anymore.
