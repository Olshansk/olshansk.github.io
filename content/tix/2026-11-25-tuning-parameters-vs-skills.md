We used to tune hyperparameters. Now we tune skills.

A hyperparameter is a number. A skill is written in language. The knobs look different, but the work is the same: change an input, measure what happens, and decide whether to keep the change.

That last step still needs high-quality, reliable ground truth. If we can’t tell a better result from a worse one, we can try a hundred numbers or rewrite the skill a hundred times and still have no idea whether we made it better.
