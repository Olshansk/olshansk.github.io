Everyone talks about A players. I think we should talk more about AAA culture.

- **Autonomy** is what you're given. You have room to decide how to do the work.
- **Agency** is what you bring. You notice what needs doing and take the first step.
- **Accountability** is what everyone expects of themselves and each other. You follow through, and you say something early when you can't.

It's possible to have one without the others. You can give people plenty of room and still have everyone wait for instructions. You can have someone who takes initiative but has to ask permission for every little decision.

When all three are there, someone can spot a problem, do something about it, and see it through without needing a manager at every step. It's amazing how far that goes.
