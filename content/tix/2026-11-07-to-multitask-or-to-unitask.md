Anything I've ever delivered or learnt of value has always been the result of unitasking.

There are periods when multitasking is necessary. It keeps things moving and is great for compounding, but not for catalyzing something or going from zero to one.

You can multitask from 1 to 100, but you have to unitask from zero to one.
