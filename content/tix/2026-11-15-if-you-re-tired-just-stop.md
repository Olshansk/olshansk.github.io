I've been getting caught up in work lately.

Sometimes you have to push through. But other times, you keep pushing, pushing, pushing. You get tired. You get frustrated. And you don't get anything done.

You could have taken a walk, done a few chores, or just taken a break. Instead, you keep trying to work.

Now you haven't gotten anything done, you're frustrated that you wasted the time, and you're even more tired.

If you're tired, just stop. Take a break.
