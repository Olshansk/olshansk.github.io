The promise of AI is to do more, but also do it more effectively and with higher quality. And not only do the things that matter. And there are times to multitask and there are times to single-task. But we should all stare more.

We should either stare at a wall, stare at a painting, stare at a tree, stare at a rock, stare at your kid, stare at your partner. So let's just stare at your closed eyelids. Stare into the abyss. It's calming. Let your brain think. Stare at the rain. Don't stare at the sun.

Staring is beautiful. Staring is calming. We should all stare more.
