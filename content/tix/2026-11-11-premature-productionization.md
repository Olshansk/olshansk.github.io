I've worked at big companies and startups. Something I've learned is that, in the short term, big companies can reward process over customer value.

In the long term, it doesn't work.

This is why you sometimes see executives from very large companies do their first startup and completely fail. What do we call this? Premature productionization.

A lot of ink has been spilled on this topic. But it's one of those things you only learn through experience.
