This book is founded on a lot of solid research. It has a ton of great references and anecdotes, and is a treasure trove of information.

With that said, I made it through about 2–3 hours of the audiobook and have decided to put it down.

This will likely be my last nonfiction, non-biographical book.

The ideas kept repeating themselves and going in circles. I felt that it could be condensed by 80–90%.

While my review is negative, I want to emphasize that it is my rating of this book for me. I felt the same way about _Thinking, Fast and Slow_.
