Fwiw, I volunteered to take a big pay cut (so it’s less impactful on the team) aside from Mike, but this sort of thing really makes you reflect on how you, as an individual (not relative to the market), brings returns to the org.

In short, you should feel comfortable to ask for w/e you’re worth as long as you’re  bringing more value up top.
