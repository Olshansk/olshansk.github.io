Pushing yourself to the limit

Some people need others to push them to the limits. Others can do it themselves.

Is it a lack of feeling pain, or willing to endure it? Should you push yourself or should you recover. The answer is always a delicate balance with tradeoffs and context.

Almost every sport I’ve done needed with injury. Boxing was a beat up face and likely a small concussion, wrestling was a dislocated shoulder, basketball was a sprained ankle, skateboarding was a board up the ass, snowboarding was a broken ankle, Olympic weighting was a meniscus tear and herniated disc.

My startups always end with burnout. You gotta push through the work, but also learn and adapt.

I can push, but I need to recover. Unfortunately, I need an external signal t recover.

People conflate smart with trusting. I did well in school because I studied the fuxk out of tests. I do well in presentations because I rehearse them 20 times. I do panels because I went to improve class.

We focus on how one looks because it’s an easy tell if you’re healthy or not. Being unfit is a clear signal, the mental one is harder to find.
