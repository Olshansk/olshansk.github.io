  * **Innovate** : Drive a technical innovation that moves humanity and transcends my lifetime

  * **Learn** : Never stop growing, learning, and trying to solve technical (or physical) challenges that I couldn’t the year prior.

  * **Socialize** : Be surrounded by people much smarter than me who are very driven and motivated to innovate (e.g. Andrej Karpathy, John Carmack)

  * **Recognition** : Have the social capital to be able to bring my own ideas and others’ ideas to light.

  * **Money (invest)** : Have enough money to be able to self finance personal capital expensive projects (e.g. bio tech related), and lead rounds of other founders being capital extensive projects (e.g. energy, VR, supply chains, etc…)

  * **Money (self)** : Have enough money whereby I don’t ever have to spend time thinking of how to optimize on costs on buying a new house, a new car, send my kids to college, going on a family trip, etc…

  * **Legacy** : Money, knowledge, etc..

  * **Useful** : Moreso than being successful, I want to be useful.
