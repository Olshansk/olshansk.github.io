## Health and cybersecurity

- Listening to [Tim Ferriss talk about trying to prevent Alzheimer's](https://tim.blog/2026/11/12/qa-with-tim-how-to-get-unstuck-transcript/), I was reminded why getting ahead of a problem matters. Treating an established disease is much harder than addressing risks early. That doesn't mean nothing can be done after onset, or that prevention guarantees avoiding Alzheimer's.
- Then I heard [Wiz CTO Ami Luttwak on the a16z podcast](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/every-day-is-zero-day-wiz-cto-ami-luttwak/id842818711?i=1000801711272), and had the same takeaway in a completely different context: the best attack to deal with is the one you prevent. Scan for external risks and improve your environment before something goes wrong. The same idea applies to health.
