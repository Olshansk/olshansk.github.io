I recently spent a week in San Francisco, catching up with friends and reflecting on the experience. It was really interesting to see how different everyone’s life is.

## Working notes

- **Friday:** I met up with an old researcher friend who has been working at OpenAI for about four years.
- **Saturday:** I met up with someone I described in the dictation as “an old Twitter.” He’s been running a business for ten years. It’s doing well, has 15 people, and he’s 50 years old and really happy. It’s on a very different scale in the fundraising industry.
- **Saturday night:** I met my dad’s music teacher. He’s in his 80s, still really fit, and can lift, exercise, and do a lot of other things. He’s at a very different stage of life and is really happy in his own way. We talked about his trans granddaughter. The dictation also mentions “every single problem, health trouble imaginable”; who that refers to still needs clarifying.
- **Sunday:** I met up with a friend who is the founder or co-founder of a multi-billion-dollar company. His wife is a senior executive at a very large, hundred-billion-dollar company.

All of these encounters come together in an interesting way: everyone has their own problems, and everyone is really happy in their own completely different life.
