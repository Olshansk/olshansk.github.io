# Old Rough Notes

    * Tx fees are not an issue

    * Token sale price comes from validators selling to cover fees - that’s a very small fraction for us

    * Circular dependency with a centralized RPC for a sequencer - the reason we have consensus is to avoid this

    * There are tokenomic reasons, ethical reasons, practical reasons

# Axioms

  * Our goal is to enable decentralized gateways

  * Decentralized gateways cannot depend on a gateway

  * We are not solving the problem of weak subjectivity

  * The list of gateways is achieved through social consensus and updated through economic consensus

  * Validators are like gateyways → we’re extending the concept
