I’ve felt down over the last few days. I’ve been overworking myself. Going to events, drinking a little, writing a little, spending time in nature, and catching up with people—both deep relationships and people I’ve known for a long time—have brought more positive energy into my life.

There’s real value in **bidirectional positive energy**: receiving positivity and exuding it yourself. It’s not karma—it’s positivity.
