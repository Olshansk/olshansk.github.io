## Working notes

- Open with yesterday’s experience of seeing the work in person. It was really interesting; the specific context still needs clarifying.
- One question I asked in a conversation was: “What is something you’ve changed your mind on over the last year?” His answer was **the ability to let go**. I think that’s very important, and I think I can do it in parts. That might be a separate piece later.
- The focus here is **understanding the other person’s incentives**, then reframing either the situation or the outcome you’re offering—specifically when working with a client.
- As we were closing out a few renewal and upsell items, I kept pushing: “Let’s get you more value. Let’s get you more value.” I didn’t realize in the moment that the other person was defensive. During that call, the goal wasn’t to create more value; it was to check a box.
- That’s not ideal, but there’s a time and place for it. I had to accept that for the moment, and it’s something that comes up with a lot of clients.
- Understanding and reframing don’t mean accepting, and they don’t mean challenging. They mean **time-bound, ongoing understanding**. I’m not talking about crossing boundaries, but recognizing that the situation may be less than ideal.
