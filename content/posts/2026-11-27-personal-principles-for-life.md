The past year has felt like a rollercoaster. I won't get into the details in this post, but I will say that it's been filled with both ups and downs. It's never a dull moment.

Knowing myself as someone who has very high highs and very low lows, even when things are "stable," this past year intensified the ride.

Three separate conversations with a few friends recently reminded me of how important it is to have principles to live by. Not "right" or "wrong" principles, but the principles you choose to lead your life with.

One of my friends mentioned how leaders make decisions quickly and smart people learn new domains fast. This hit deeply as someone who likes to understand things deeply and slowly.

Another one of my friends pointed out that the reason I struggle to make decisions is that there isn't an underlying principle I can apply to different parts of life. Once you've been honing the same principle for a decade or two, you can use it as a shortcut or fallback when confronted with something new.

> [6:59 AM, 11/7/2026] Rahul Goel: No secret man, just basic stuff. I love space, I focus on the long term, I like junk food, I don’t overthink or prestress about things I can’t control, I love my country, and now I am so grateful for and most of all love my family.
>
> [7:01 AM, 11/7/2026] Rahul Goel: I don’t have a master plan but I do have a vague vision of the future. I really like my friends and want to stay close to them, and I like hiring unproblematic people who just care deeply about doing good work and have good hearts.
