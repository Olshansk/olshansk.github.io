Reading notes and reflections by Daniel Olshansky.

Source: [Shipping is the foundation](https://seangoedecke.com/shipping-is-the-foundation/) — Sean Goedecke.

A couple of interesting quotes, with mini reflections.

> But all of these rest on the foundation of being able to ship.

Easy. You need to ship, but also reship if you shipped the wrong thing.

The author made a lot of analogies to StarCraft, Magic, etc. Maybe this is why Elon is such a big gamer. It’s an easy way to bias toward action.

---

> Half the point of managers asking you for things is to do an end-run around that process: it’s the fast, illegible alternative to going through ordinary channels.

This is VERY true, and I never thought of it that way.

---

At the very end, the author referenced Richard Hamming’s “You and Your Research.” This is one of my favorite pieces of writing, and I even wrote a [small post about it](/posts/you-and-your-research/) in the past.

Given his high praise for it, I’ll add these to my backlog too:

- [Nobody knows how large software products work](https://seangoedecke.com/nobody-knows-how-software-products-work/)
- [Aggro is the Foundation](https://radimentary.wordpress.com/2022/11/07/aggro-is-the-foundation/)
