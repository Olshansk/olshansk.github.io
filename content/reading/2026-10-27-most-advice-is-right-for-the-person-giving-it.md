> Steve Jobs gave a famous speech at Stanford, where he told graduates that the only way to do great work is to love what you do, and that if you haven’t found your passion yet, you should keep looking. Interestingly, Mark Cuban says the opposite: don’t follow your passion, follow your effort, because you’ll end up loving whatever you get good at.”

— Rohan Mahtani, [Most advice is right for the person giving it](https://coached.com/essays/most-advice-is-right-for-the-person-giving-it/), Coached Weekly, September 5, 2026.
