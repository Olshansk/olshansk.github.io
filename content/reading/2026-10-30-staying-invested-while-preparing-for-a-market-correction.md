Always consider going with the S&P, a Vanguard index, or individual stocks. You always want to prepare to have cash during a market correction.

But the best idea is to invest in something like the market or a Vanguard index. Then, when the market does crash, you take the loss and reinvest.

That gives you an opportunity to have a 10x upside, even after a 20% downside.
