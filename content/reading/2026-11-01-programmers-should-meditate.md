I met a monk with the original shrine in Nara. My mind was racing, and I noticed how unshallow my breaths were. I put it aside that I couldn't focus.

Running helps you lose focus. Sports and judo help you focus. Flow in programming is how people used to focus. Writing by hand helps you focus.

I think we're going to see an epidemic from social media and AI in terms of their impact on mental health, more so than anything else.
