Sean Goedecke is increasingly writing high-quality posts on pretty much everything.

This one is on rewrites and when—or if—a rewrite to C, Rust, or assembly creates value.

His key point is that rewrites are fun and can create value, but you need to redesign the software.

To me, it was a good reminder to tackle problems from first principles.
