A short miniseries capturing the story of a short presidency.

It's fascinating to see the different forms and shapes a presidency can take.

It's a clear reminder that there is no straight path to entrepreneurship. Everyone does it in their own way.

James Garfield was the epitome of a classic leader: an eloquent speaker who invigorated crowds. But he didn't want it.

Chester Arthur was a completely different type of leader who found himself in a place he never expected and adapted along the way. He also didn't want it.

Whether you want it, seek it, achieve it, get it, or try to reject it, true leadership isn't a skill. It's individuality.

I'm glad Netflix makes stories like this known.
